Overview

This document provides a comprehensive analysis of Bridge2AI (B2AI) consortium onboarding data, examining team composition, temporal evolution, institutional diversity, and participation patterns across data generation projects (DGPs) and cores. The analysis focuses on understanding the structure and dynamics of this large-scale team science initiative.

Data generation projects:

  • Voice: Precision public health (B2AI Voice)
  • AI-READi: Salutogenesis
  • CHoRUS: AI/ML for clinical care
  • CM4AI: Functional genomics

Key observations

Temporal patterns:

  • The consortium shows distinct growth phases, with peak onboarding during specific months
  • DGPs show different onboarding timelines, likely reflecting project initiation dates
  • Career stage composition has evolved over time

Institutional diversity:

  • Strong representation from academia, with significant government and non-profit participation
  • Top institutions contribute multiple members, but distribution is relatively broad
  • Cross-institutional collaboration potential is high within each DGP

Team composition:

  • Balanced representation of career stages, from trainees to senior investigators
  • Substantial cross-DGP and cross-core participation, facilitating knowledge transfer
  • Institution type diversity enables academic-government-industry partnerships

Expertise landscape:

  • Broad range of expertise areas covering data science, biomedical research, ethics, and standards
  • Shared expertise across DGPs creates natural collaboration bridges
  • Interdisciplinary teams with complementary skills

Discussion and future analyses

Data sufficiency for team composition analysis

The current dataset provides:

Sufficient data for:

  • ✓ Institutional diversity analysis
  • ✓ Geographic distribution assessment
  • ✓ Temporal evolution of team size and composition
  • ✓ Basic career stage classification (from trainee, srKey, and degrees)
  • ✓ Role distribution (senior key vs. trainees vs. other)
  • ✓ Expertise area representation
  • ✓ Cross-DGP and cross-core collaboration potential
  • ✓ Institution type distribution (academia, government, industry, etc.)

Limited data for:

  • ⚠ Precise career stage classification (would benefit from academic rank, years since degree)
  • ⚠ Actual collaboration patterns vs. potential (would need network data, co-authorship)
  • ⚠ Team effectiveness outcomes (would need performance metrics, deliverables)
  • ⚠ Publication-based success metrics (not in onboarding data)
  • ⚠ Longitudinal engagement tracking (only onboarding, no off-boarding data)

Recommended additional data for success metrics

To evaluate “successful collaborations” and refine team composition analyses:

  1. Collaboration network data: (w/ Sean, Jake)
    • Co-authorship on publications
    • Shared datasets or tools developed
    • Joint grant submissions
    • Cross-DGP working group participation
  2. Productivity metrics:
    • Publications per team/DGP
    • Datasets generated and released
    • Software tools developed
    • Standards proposed/adopted
  3. Career tracking:
    • Academic rank (for faculty)
    • Years since terminal degree
    • Current vs. starting career stage
    • Career advancement during/after B2AI
  4. Engagement indicators:
    • Meeting attendance patterns
    • Contribution frequency (commits, documents, etc.)
    • Leadership roles taken over time
    • Mentor-mentee relationships
  5. Team dynamics:
    • Team satisfaction surveys (like those by Goldie’s evaluation team)
    • Perceived collaboration quality
    • Communication effectiveness ratings
    • Conflict resolution success
  6. Impact metrics:
    • Citation counts for B2AI-related publications
    • Dataset reuse by external researchers
    • Tool adoption rates
    • Standards implementation

Technical notes

Data sources and methods

Primary data: anonymized B2AI onboarding data combining the Nov 2025 onboarding form and the Sept 2026 roster (file: B2AI_onboarding_combined_anonymized.csv)

Column usage: - Career stage inferred from: trainee, srKey, degrees - DGP participation from: Voice_*, AI-READi_*, CHoRUS_*, CM4AI_* module columns - Core participation from: Admin_core-deprecate, Ethics_core, Teaming_core_depr, Standards_core_depr, Tools_core_deprecate, SWD_core_deprecate - Institution type classified from: primaryAffilName using keyword matching

Limitations

  1. Onboarding data only: This represents when people joined, not ongoing engagement or departure
  2. Self-reported: Expertise and other fields rely on participant self-reporting
  3. Career stage inference: Career stages are inferred from available fields; some may be misclassified
  4. Institution type classification: Automated classification based on keywords; may have errors
  5. No outcome data: Cannot assess collaboration success without additional metrics
