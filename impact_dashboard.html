This page tracks the consortium’s impact measures in one place. For each measure it shows the data that exists today and flags the measures nobody records yet. Publication, citation and collaboration counts come from the bridge2aikg analysis of 413 award-verified publications (corpus snapshot 2026-07-22). Field-normalized citation impact (NIH iCite), clinical-citation flags, and papers that mention Bridge2AI (Europe PMC) come from the bridge2ai_eval_data spreadsheet (iCite records through 2025-06-28), which also serves as a cross-check on the publication list. Code repository metrics come from the GitHub API. Membership and team-science numbers come from the onboarding export and the baseline and 6-month surveys in this repository. Measures with no existing source are filled from the templates in data/impact/manual/.
