Summary

This analysis examines changes in collaboration patterns, resource utilization, and team dynamics within the Bridge2AI consortium between baseline and 6-month follow-up surveys. The analysis identifies key trends in participation, internal collaboration quality, and emerging challenges to inform the NIH Collaboration Improvement Plan.

Key insights and recommendations

1. Participation changes
- Largest increase: AI-READi DGP (+37 participants)
- Largest decrease: Bridge2AI Center (-14 participants)
2. Internal collaboration changes
- No statistically significant changes in internal collaboration metrics
3. Recommendations for collaboration improvement plan
- Focus on groups with declining internal collaboration scores
- Address specific challenge areas identified in follow-up survey
- Leverage high-performing groups as mentors for struggling groups
- Monitor participation trends in future surveys
